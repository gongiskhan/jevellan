# Shared conversation, decision and correction indexes

**Live:** local HTTP, SQLite migration and owner-ledger persistence. **Simulated:** member devices and providers. **Not run:** live second-device execution or successful Jev classification.

The hub now keeps the slim conversation and decision records required by BRIEF §5.6–5.7. Conversation indexes contain identity, title, project, owner, state, update time and current model/effort. Decision indexes contain identity, conversation, work, time, selected action/model/effort with their sources, notices and outcome. Requests, constraints, full decision packets, probabilities, memory selections, messages and native runtime details remain in owner-local history. The conversation-list response is now `conversations-list-v2`; the compiled web application reads that shape.

At hub startup, existing full conversation and decision documents migrate together in a transaction. Invalid stored data aborts startup and rolls back the migration rather than leaving a partially converted set. Owner ledgers and their full projections are unchanged. Correction records retain the existing brief-required structured feedback, and both the decision packet and the Why correction lookup now await shared index access.

Authenticated `/hub/mesh/indexes` operations publish owner-bound records and page through conversation/correction lists. A device cannot replace another conversation owner or change its project. Each index record has a durable cursor tied to its owner's ledger event sequence. Identical replay returns its existing acknowledgement; older delayed requests cannot overwrite newer data. Conflicting content at the same event is refused. Record changes and acknowledgement cursors commit in one transaction. Member reads contact the hub; there is no authoritative member database or fallback list.

Index delivery is asynchronous and coalesces pending updates by record. The durable owner ledger is its recovery source: startup reconstructs the current indexes without repeating a decision or launching a step. A successful acknowledgement removes only the exact pending update sent, preserving any newer replacement queued during the request. Failed delivery retries after thirty seconds. A conversation index is delivered before its child decision/correction records. Shutdown waits for in-flight delivery and leaves undelivered history available for startup recovery. A slow or unavailable index service cannot hold the running process's event stream or erase its completed local outcome.
